  <name>Task 1: PingRetryPolicy</name>
  <files>src/Queue/PingRetryPolicy.swift</files>
  <action>`struct PingRetryPolicy: Sendable, Equatable` with `static let standard = PingRetryPolicy()`, and three members:
- `func delay(afterAttempts attemptsMade: Int) -> TimeInterval` — the wait before the NEXT attempt, given how many have already been made. The FORMULA is the definition: `min(30 * pow(2, attemptsMade - 1), 3600)`, in whole seconds. It yields, for attempts 1 through 9: 30, 60, 120, 240, 480, 960, 1920, 3600 (30×2⁷ = 3840, capped), 3600. Note 7 → **1920**, not 1800 — an earlier draft of this plan carried a hand-written table that disagreed with its own formula at that one point, and the table was the thing that was wrong. `attemptsMade <= 0` returns 30 rather than trapping.
- `func nextAttemptDate(afterAttempts attemptsMade: Int, now: Date) -> Date` — `now.addingTimeInterval(delay(afterAttempts:))`. `now` is a parameter; the type never calls `Date()`.
- `func hasGivenUp(firstAttemptAt: Date, now: Date) -> Bool` — true once `now >= firstAttemptAt + 7 days` (`604_800`). Time-based, not count-based, on purpose: a phone in airplane mode for two days makes no attempts at all, so a count-based limit would give up in minutes on the one day it was briefly online and never at all on the days it was not.
- `static let gaveUpReason: String` — a finished sentence, what happened and what to do next (DESIGN.md), e.g. "This ping could not be delivered after a week of retries and has been given up. Check the webhook URL in Settings, then tap I'm here again." No status code alone, no bare status word.
Document at the top, in prose, that both the 7-day horizon and the classifier's treatment of non-401/403 4xx as permanent are BACKSTOP decisions this phase's requirements do not settle (see this plan's `backstop_truths`), so a later reader does not mistake either for a derived rule. No `Date()`, no `URLSession`, no `FileManager` anywhere in the file.</action>
