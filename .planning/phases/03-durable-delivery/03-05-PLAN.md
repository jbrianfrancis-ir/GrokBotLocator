<objective>
Bound one send attempt in time and in bytes. A drain that runs inside a background refresh task
gets seconds, not minutes, and an attempt that can hang for a minute is a drain that never
finishes — the two defects .planning/LEARNINGS.md records, fixed where the queue needs them.
</objective>
