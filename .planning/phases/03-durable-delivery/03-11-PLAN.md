<objective>
`BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers` is an array, which `INFOPLIST_KEY_*` build settings cannot
express. Move the app target to an explicit XcodeGen-authored Info.plist, losing nothing.
</objective>

<context>
project.yml — the `GrokBotLocator` target currently sets `GENERATE_INFOPLIST_FILE: YES` and four
`INFOPLIST_KEY_*` settings; each carries a comment explaining why it exists (the launch-screen
one in particular: without it iOS runs the app letterboxed).
GrokBotLocator.entitlements shows the established build-setting-reference pattern,
`$(AppIdentifierPrefix)$(PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER)`.
ARCHITECTURE.md Forbidden — "Committing DEVELOPMENT_TEAM, a bundle id, or a provisioning
profile." That is why the identifier below is a reference and not a string.
.planning/DECISIONS.md 2026-09-10 14:26 — "no version change"; see the note in Task 1.
XcodeGen 2.46.0 is the pinned generator. Gate: SMOKE_DERIVED_DATA=build/dd-03-11 ./scripts/smoke.sh.
</context>
