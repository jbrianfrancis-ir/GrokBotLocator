<context>
.planning/DESIGN.md is binding — primary action full-width in the bottom third, 28pt label,
>= 88pt tall, opaque fill; status rows always symbol + word + colour; Liquid Glass on chrome
ONLY (navigation and toolbars), never behind body text, a field, or the primary action, and
opaque under Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast; **no text over photographic or variable
backgrounds**; errors say what happened and what to do next in body-size text on screen; the
ping outcome is announced, not just rendered; Dynamic Type to AX5 with no truncation, clipping
or overlap; nothing below 17pt.

**Why the title is not in the chrome bar.** Two DESIGN.md rules pull against each other for a
custom translucent header: "Chrome: navigation and toolbars only" invites a title bar, while
"no text over variable backgrounds" and the 7:1 contrast floor cannot be satisfied by any text
sitting over scrolling content seen through a material — there is no fixed background to measure
the ratio against. Independently, the arithmetic rules it out anyway, on today's curves and with no
curve change anywhere in this phase: `.screenTitle` renders ~64pt at AX5 (DECISIONS 2026-09-10
14:26 measured 70pt rendered), and an unbreakable 14-character app name needs 400pt+ of width
against ~290-410pt of portrait content width. The 2026-09-10 15:30 decision left DSTypography
untouched, so that number is final for this phase rather than a floor that a curve choice could
raise. So this plan puts the Settings
control alone in the chrome bar — no text to contrast-check, no text to truncate — and the
screen title in the scrolling content as a wrapping multi-word string, which satisfies both
rules at once. DESIGN.md is NOT amended; it does not need to be.

.planning/ROADMAP.md "Carried into phase 02 from phase 01" — the three deferred checks this
screen makes reachable. Plan 02-13 closes them on screen, together with REQ-12's audit.

the precedent for a screen title in scrolling content.
